Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you install or use our application ("App") in connection with your Shopify-powered store.

1. Personal Information the App Collects

When you install the App, we are automatically able to access certain types of information from your Shopify account:

  • Store Information: Store domain, name, and basic settings.
  • Customer Data: Customer tags and customer IDs to determine customer group segmentation for discounts.
  • Product Data: Product collections, product variants, and pricing information to apply automated discount rules.

We do not store or collect customers' sensitive personal payment information (like credit card numbers), as all checkout and payment processing are handled securely by Shopify.

2. How Do We Use Your Personal Information?

We use the information collected from you and your customers to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App's core functionality.
  • Troubleshoot, support, and improve the App's performance.
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations.

3. Sharing Your Personal Information

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your information with third parties, except as necessary to comply with laws, protect our rights, or with your explicit consent.

4. Your Rights

If you are a European resident, you have the right to access personal information we hold about you and to ask that your personal information be corrected, updated, or deleted.
